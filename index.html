How to link this to your physical NFC card / tag
  1. Install 'NFC Tools': Download the free NFC Tools app on Android (Play Store) or iPhone (App Store).
  2. Copy URL: Tap the share/copy button at the top to copy this website link.
  3. Write Tag: Open NFC Tools > Tap Write > Add a record > URL / URI > Paste this website URL.
  4. Scan Card: Tap Write and hold your physical NFC card or keychain tag against the back of your phone. Done! Now anyone who taps your card will instantly open this page!
